    Teams aren't worth the same

    One flat price leaves money on the table for the best teams and leaves the weak spots unsold.

    Fees come out of every sale

    Platform and payment fees are taken from the sale price, so they're easy to underestimate when you price by feel.

    Margins are thin

    One badly priced break can wipe out the profit from several good ones.

    From new case to priced spot list in two minutes

    1. Enter your numbers

      Box cost, shipping, the profit you want, and your real fees: the percent and the fixed charge on every order. Every number is yours.

    2. Rate each team

      Mark every spot Top, Mid or Low and prices update with each tap. See your profit if the break only fills 70, 80 or 90 percent, before you buy the box.

    3. Go live

      Paste the prices into your listing. During the show, tap each spot as it sells and watch your profit build.

    See what each break really made

    Tap spots as they sell, or import your sales afterward. BreakMath shows real profit next to the plan, by break, by product and by month, and flags the products that lose money.
