    Teams aren't worth the same

    One flat price leaves money on the table for the best teams and leaves the weak spots unsold.

    Fees come out of every sale

    Platform and payment fees are taken from the sale price, so they're easy to underestimate when you price by feel.

    Margins are thin

    One badly priced break can wipe out the profit from several good ones.

    From new case to priced spot list in two minutes

    1. Enter your numbers

      Box cost, shipping, fees and the profit you want. Every number is yours. BreakMath never guesses card prices.

    2. Rate each team

      Mark every spot Top, Mid or Low. Prices update with each tap, and you can see how far below list you can sell before losing money.

    3. Copy your spot list

      Paste the prices into your listing and go live knowing what the break will make.

    See what each break really made

    After the stream, enter what the spots sold for. BreakMath shows real profit next to the plan, by break, by product and by month, so you stop running products that lose money.
