Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version. The breaks you plan and save stay in your own browser, not on our servers. If you pay for Pro, Stripe handles the payment and we never see your card number. If you give us your email, we use it to send product updates. We count visits without cookies. We don't sell your information and we don't run advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

BreakMath ("BreakMath", "we", "us") offers a spot pricing and profit tracking tool for people who run card breaks. This policy covers this website and the BreakMath app. For privacy questions or requests, email kushshah1515@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address, when you sign up for product updates. We save it with the time you signed up and the ad campaign or website that sent you to us.
  • Payment and subscription details, when you buy Pro. Stripe collects your name, email address, billing country and card details. We can see your name, email, plan, payment history and the last four digits and brand of your card in our Stripe account. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Anything you send us, when you email us, including your email address and the contents of your message.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit statistics. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies. It records the page viewed, the referring site or campaign, browser and operating system, screen size, and country or region. It does not keep your full IP address and does not build a profile of you across sites. It also counts sign-ups and upgrades, without receiving any email address.
  • Server logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of requests, including IP addresses, for security and to operate its service.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your breaks. Everything you enter in the app, including products, costs, fees, team lists, prices, results and profit history, is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it, recover it or move it between devices. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use the backup option in the app.
  • Your plan. Whether Pro is unlocked is also stored in your browser.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, government identifiers or sensitive personal information. We do not use advertising pixels or cross-site tracking.

3. How we use information

  • To provide Pro, take payment through Stripe, and handle billing questions, cancellations and refunds.
  • To send a confirmation email when you sign up for updates, and occasional emails about new features, pricing and requests for feedback.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To understand how many people visit, where they come from and whether they use the product, so we can improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and working, and to prevent abuse.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal bases (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Legal reasons, if we believe disclosure is required by law or legal process, or needed to protect the rights, property or safety of BreakMath, our users or others.
  • Business changes, if BreakMath is reorganized, merged or sold. The recipient would have to honor this policy or tell you about any change.
  • With your permission, in any other case.

6. How long we keep information

  • Update emails list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your address.
  • Payment records: as long as you subscribe and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Stripe keeps its own records under its privacy policy.
  • Your breaks: they stay in your browser until you delete them or clear your browser data.
  • Messages you send us: as long as needed to handle your request and keep a record of it.
  • Visit statistics: kept in aggregated form that does not identify you.
