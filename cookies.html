Cookie Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

The short version. BreakMath does not set cookies, so there is no cookie banner to click through.

1. Cookies

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. This site does not set any, for advertising, analytics or anything else.

2. Analytics without cookies

We use Vercel Web Analytics, and may use GoatCounter, to count visits. Neither uses cookies or stores anything on your device, and neither follows you across other websites. What it records is described in our Privacy Policy.

3. Browser storage

Browsers offer "local storage", which works like a cookie but is never sent to a server. The BreakMath app uses it to keep you signed in and to hold the break you are still editing. This is necessary for the app to work. Clearing it in your browser's site data settings signs you out; breaks you have saved to your account are not affected.

4. Other services your browser contacts

  • Vercel hosts the site, counts visits without cookies, and receives the technical details of each request, including your IP address.
  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores your account, saved breaks, email sign-ups and Pro interest.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typeface, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google.
  • GoatCounter receives the visit information described above.
  • Google may receive your email address if you sign up for updates, if we store sign-ups in Google Sheets.
  • Stripe handles checkout on its own website when you buy Pro, and sets its own cookies there for security and fraud prevention.

We do not use advertising pixels or social media trackers.

5. Your controls

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings, and you can use a content blocker to stop analytics. The site works the same either way.

6. Changes

If we start using cookies we will update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
